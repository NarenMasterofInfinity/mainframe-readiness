# Start here

We are building a tool that helps a team understand a mainframe application before making a migration plan. It reads supplied artifacts, finds connections, highlights risks and tells the team what is still unknown. It does not move or rewrite the application.

Think of it like a building inspection: the inspector studies the rooms and wiring, records what they could not access and recommends what to investigate before renovation.
